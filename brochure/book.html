Meet Alina

The Person Behind It All

Alina Vitali founded AV Events Agency in Chicago in 2019. With eight years in the wedding industry, 200+ weddings and 1000+ events behind her, she has become one of the city's most trusted wedding planners — ranked in Wezoree's Top 5 for Chicago and Top 100 in the country.

She is also a rare two-in-one: a certified wedding planner and a licensed wedding minister. That means she can plan your whole wedding and lead your ceremony — all with one caring, familiar face.
